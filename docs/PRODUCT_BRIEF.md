# Product brief — Wasel

## Product
Wasel helps distribution and wholesale businesses see operational risks earlier and make better inventory decisions.

## Initial target
Small and mid-sized distributors in Egypt, initially food/FMCG wholesalers with one or more warehouses and field sales activity.

## Primary buyer
Owner, general manager, or operations manager.

## Initial user roles
- Owner / General Manager
- Operations Manager
- Warehouse Manager
- Sales Supervisor

## MVP problem to address
Operators often need to combine information from stock sheets, sales reports, and purchase records to answer:
- Which products may run out soon?
- Which products are moving slowly or becoming overstocked?
- What should be reordered, and approximately when?
- Which figures need human review before action?

## MVP workflow
1. Import a simple CSV/Excel stock and sales export.
2. Validate and preview the data.
3. Show stock health, fast/slow movers, and potential stockout risks.
4. Explain each alert with transparent calculations and source rows.
5. Export a suggested replenishment review for a human to approve.

## AI principle
AI explains patterns and drafts recommendations; deterministic calculations produce quantities and thresholds. A human approves purchasing decisions. The system must show the data and assumptions behind every recommendation.

## Not in first MVP
- Full accounting
- Payroll / HR
- Route optimization
- GPS fleet tracking
- Payment processing
- Automatic purchase orders
- Replacing a customer's ERP
- Autonomous AI actions

## Success measures
- Time required to prepare a stock-risk report
- Number of stockout/overstock risks surfaced and confirmed by staff
- Recommendation usefulness rating
- Number of companies completing a pilot
- Paid pilot conversion

## Validation gate
Do not expand the product until at least two distributors confirm the same recurring workflow pain and one agrees to a paid pilot.
